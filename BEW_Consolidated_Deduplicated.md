## The Problem We Solve

### Stakeholders Demand Proof. We Provide It.

Investors, customers, and employees are increasingly skeptical of unverified sustainability claims. Greenwashing is widespread, while sustainability frameworks remain fragmented.

### The Challenge: Sustainability Claims Without Proof
Organizations often rely on self-reported data without independent verification, creating stakeholder doubt.

### The Gap: No Single Standard
ISO standards, ESG reporting, CSR guidelines, and other frameworks can tell different stories. Organizations struggle to demonstrate comparability and often face expensive consultants, unclear pathways, and wasted resources.

### The Solution: Independent, Credible Verification
Better Earth Workplaces provides rigorous third-party certification aligned with global standards. Impact is measured, verified, documented, and designed to be recognized by investors, regulators, customers, and other stakeholders.

**Outcome:** Investor confidence. Stakeholder trust. Competitive advantage.

## Why Choose Better Earth Workplaces

### 1. Genuinely Independent
We are not a consulting firm and do not sell remediation solutions to organizations we assess. Revenue comes from certification fees, creating alignment between our business and our credibility. Assessment and remediation remain separate.

Certification criteria are developed using peer-reviewed research, global best practices, and stakeholder input. Standards evolve with the science and focus on real environmental and social impact.

Organizations know in advance what they will be assessed on. Criteria are public, assessments are documented, and results are objective.

### 4. Recognized Worldwide

### 5. Investor-Grade Credibility

## How Certification Works

**Headline:** Your Certification Journey, Transparent & Achievable

**Core timeline:** Approximately 8–12 weeks from preparation through certification.

### Stage 1: Explore & Qualify
- Choose a certification pathway.
- Review public criteria.
- Complete a brief eligibility check.
- Typical timing: Week 1.

### Stage 2: Prepare & Gather
Organizations receive:
- Assessment framework
- Sample questionnaire
- Preparation checklist
- Estimated timeline

Evidence may include:
- Operational data such as energy, water, waste, and emissions
- Policies and procedures
- Supplier certifications and regulatory filings
- Impact metrics and outcome data

Organizations select a qualified assessor from the BEW network.

**Typical timing:** Weeks 2–4.

### Stage 3: Assessment
Depending on certification, the assessor may conduct:
- Document review
- Site verification or virtual walkthrough
- Stakeholder interviews
- Data validation
- Scoring against BEW criteria

Assessments typically take 4–6 weeks. Organizations receive regular check-ins and draft findings before the final report.

### Stage 4: Independent Review & Certification
An independent review panel verifies:
- Correct application of criteria
- Sufficiency and credibility of evidence
- Consistency of scoring
- Absence of conflicts of interest

**Typical timing:** Weeks 9–10.

Possible outcomes:
- **Certified:** Meets the standard; receives a time-bound certification badge, dashboard access, and directory listing.
- **Certified with Conditions:** Substantially meets the standard with minor gaps; typically 6–12 months for remediation and follow-up verification.
- **Not Yet Certified:** Gaps must be addressed before reapplication, typically after 12 months.

### Stage 5: Ongoing Compliance & Reporting
Certification is described as valid for 3 years, with annual recertification/compliance checks. Annual checks are lighter-touch and typically take 2–4 weeks.

Certified organizations receive:
- Digital certification badge
- Public directory listing
- Impact dashboard
- Reporting tools
- Partner network access
- Press-release support

## FAQ

### Eligibility & Fit

**Is my organization too small?**  
No. Certifications are designed for organizations of all sizes, with scope, effort, and pricing adjusted accordingly.

**Do I need to operate in a specific country?**  
No. BEW is presented as a global authority. Remote assessment or training-partner support may be possible where local assessor coverage is unavailable.

**What if we already have ISO, B Corp, or another certification?**  
Existing certifications and third-party data can support the assessment but do not replace BEW's independent criteria.

### Assessment & Process

**What happens if we do not pass?**  
You receive detailed feedback on gaps and can reapply after remediation, typically after 12 months.

**Can we appeal?**  
Yes. An appeal can be requested within 30 days. An independent panel reviews the assessment and issues a final decision.

**How long is certification valid?**  
The source states 3 years, with annual recertification/compliance checks.

**Can we work with a consultant during assessment?**  
Yes. Consultants may assist with data collection, gap analysis, or remediation, but the assessment itself must remain independent.

### Costs

**Are payment plans available?**  
Yes. The source describes a typical three-installment structure: application, assessment start, and certification decision.

**What about multiple certifications?**  
Organizations pursuing two or more certifications may receive 10–15% savings.

### Using Certification

**Can we use the BEW badge?**  
Yes. It may be used on websites, reports, packaging, and marketing materials, subject to usage guidelines. The badge should link to the public certification record.

**Can BEW certification appear in investor reports?**  
Yes. The source positions certification for ESG disclosures, annual reports, and investor decks.

**What if certification is lost?**  
The badge can no longer be used while certification is inactive. The directory remains transparent about status and date, and reapplication may occur the following year.
